01 -- What it is

Court- and regulator-grade evidence for AI-agent and audit workflows.

ActaSeal sits in front of money-touching agent actions as a policy gateway. Every decision it makes -- allow, block, or route to a human approver -- is written to a tamper-evident ledger and returned to the caller as an Ed25519-signed receipt. The receipt, not ActaSeal's word, is the evidence: it can be checked offline, years later, by a party who has never run ActaSeal and does not trust the vendor.

Below is an excerpt of a real receipt minted by the gateway's own default refund policy, binding the merchant's terms document into the signed payload (terms_hash) so the terms in force at decision time cannot be swapped after the fact.

{
  "decision": "ALLOW",
  "reason_code": "POLICY_ALLOW",
  "policy_hash": "9c54da05406442e71a1afe92a5c548f3b30758f9825bfb71ed201a512cff3dce",
  "action_hash": "14c55c4f8792690b37b63600042944122cccf92b931d68b009fe7c07ac627140",
  "ledger_entry_hash": "96f7b13d7ae398134635498a7ffc670750e0c791f243e81b7bfcb8ab31753f81",
  "terms_hash": "5bc04174974fc7cd5d97dd6fe17e79549004f713f1d1e7956aa7fd8bf096259b",
  "signature": "af9de4214f73e89b6a6d9b252bed62f355a05ccbf12097921aa890cab3d8eb4...",
  "algorithm": "ed25519"
}

Verify a receipt like this yourself, offline, using only the public key and the standalone verifier -- no ActaSeal account or network call required.

02 -- Who it stops what for

Three groups, three specific consequences avoided

Audit firms
PCAOB AS 1215 archive integrity (workpapers cannot be altered after the documentation completion date without it being provable) and QC 1000 / ISQM 1 monitoring and remediation evidence for the firm's own quality-management system.
Legal hold and med-mal
FRCP 37(e) preservation of electronically stored information when litigation is reasonably anticipated, and an FRE 902(13)/(14) self-authenticating certification package for records generated or copied by an electronic process.
Agent-commerce disputes
An LCP (Legal Context Protocol) binding profile that ties the terms a merchant agent relied on into the signed decision receipt itself, so "what terms were actually in force" is not a matter of testimony after the dispute starts.

These are the standards ActaSeal's exports are built to address. ActaSeal is not certified under any of them; certification, where applicable, is the customer's own process.
