01 -- What it is

Proof of what your AI agents did — that your customer can verify without trusting us.

If you sell AI agents to enterprises, your customer will ask what the agent did and who allowed it. ActaSeal sits in front of the actions that move money. It decides each one against your policy -- allow, block, or send to a person for approval -- records the decision in a tamper-evident ledger, and signs a receipt for it. Your customer checks that receipt on their own machine, with an open-source verifier, years later if need be. Nothing in the check depends on us, or on you.

Below is an excerpt of a real receipt for an allowed refund. It binds the terms document in force at the time into the signed payload (terms_hash), so those terms cannot be swapped after the fact.

{
  "decision": "ALLOW",
  "reason_code": "POLICY_ALLOW",
  "policy_hash": "9c54da05406442e71a1afe92a5c548f3b30758f9825bfb71ed201a512cff3dce",
  "action_hash": "14c55c4f8792690b37b63600042944122cccf92b931d68b009fe7c07ac627140",
  "ledger_entry_hash": "96f7b13d7ae398134635498a7ffc670750e0c791f243e81b7bfcb8ab31753f81",
  "terms_hash": "5bc04174974fc7cd5d97dd6fe17e79549004f713f1d1e7956aa7fd8bf096259b",
  "signature": "af9de4214f73e89b6a6d9b252bed62f355a05ccbf12097921aa890cab3d8eb4...",
  "algorithm": "ed25519"
}

Verify a receipt like this yourself, offline, using only the public key and the standalone verifier -- no ActaSeal account or network call required.

02 -- Also used for

Records that have to be provably unaltered

The same signed, independently checkable record is used where a file must be shown not to have changed after a date: audit workpaper archives under PCAOB AS 1215, and records under legal hold, with an FRE 902(13)/(14) certification package for records produced by an electronic process.

These are the standards ActaSeal's exports are built to address. ActaSeal is not certified under any of them; certification, where applicable, is the customer's own process.
