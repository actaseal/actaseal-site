What it does for you

It decides before the money moves.
Each payment your agent attempts is checked against rules you set, before it runs. It goes ahead, is stopped, or is sent to a person for approval.
It leaves a record nobody can quietly change.
What was decided, and why, is written down in a form that shows any later change.
Your customer can check it without you.
They do not need your account, your software or your word.
