Your AI agent issues refunds. Can you show why each one was given?

When a seller, a restaurant or your own finance team asks why a refund went out, the agent's own log is the agent's own account. A refund audit checks your recent AI-issued refunds against your written refund rules and tells you which ones fell outside them, and how much money was at issue.

Book a 20-min call

What the audit finds

  • Refunds above the amount your rules allow the agent to give on its own, with no approval recorded.
  • Refunds above your hard limit.
  • More than one refund on the same order.
  • Refunds that add up to more than the order was worth.
  • Customers who received refunds again and again.

Each finding names the refunds involved and the money at issue.

How it runs

  • You share an export of recent refunds (order, customer, amount, date) and your refund rules.
  • Nothing connects to your systems, and no one needs access to them.
  • You get a report within five working days, sealed so that any later change to it shows.

What comes after

  1. Refund audit: five working days, on an export. Shows what already went out.
  2. Paid pilot: three weeks, in your test environment. Each refund is checked before the money moves.
  3. In production: the same checks on every refund your agent issues.
