Refunds with no GST credit note lose that GST after 30 November.

When you refund an order, the GST you paid on the sale comes back only through a credit note. A credit note for last year's sales has to be declared by 30 November after that year ends, or the annual return date if that comes first (CGST Act, section 34(2)). After that, the GST on a refund with no credit note stays paid.

Free, in your browser, nothing uploaded. Add your credit note register in the third box.

What you need

  • Your refunds export (Razorpay, Shopify or your returns tool), with the order number of each refund.
  • Your credit note register from Tally, Zoho Books, Shopify or Unicommerce, with the order number and the value of each credit note.

GSTR-1 is not the file to use: small intra-state sales to consumers are reported there as one total, so it does not list their credit notes one by one. Your books' register does.

What you get

  • Each order you refunded with no credit note, or with less credited than refunded, and the amount.
  • The same check for refunds that broke your rules, and refunds you accepted but have not paid.

If the two files do not share an order number, the check says so instead of calling every refund uncredited. Your accountant issues the credit notes; this tells them which ones are missing.
