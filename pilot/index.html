A paid pilot

A paid pilot puts ActaSeal in front of one of your agents' payment flows, in your own environment, so you can see the result with your own data before you decide anything else.

What you get to see

  • A decision for each payment before it runs: go ahead, stop, or send to a person.
  • A record of each decision that shows any later change.
  • A way for your customer to check a disputed payment for themselves.

Scope, timing and terms are agreed on a call.
