Who is accountable

ActaSeal is built by Xavier Goshi, its founder, who is accountable for the product, the published verifier, and everything written on this site.

Xavier has a background in computer science and in sales: he builds the product, and he has sat across the table from the people who have to sign off on a purchase, learning what they need to see before they say yes. Before ActaSeal he co-founded Karmas, a platform connecting people with NGOs, where he onboarded 25 NGOs across three Indian cities.

He writes the standards and research behind ActaSeal in the open: an IETF draft on proving payment disputes and published research on how tests for agent payment records miss rules. Outside work, he reads his way through the classics.

What that means for a buyer

Whether the evidence works does not depend on who made it. The verifier is published separately under the Apache License 2.0 and runs offline, so nothing about the product's correctness depends on this company still existing.

Standards work

The design decisions behind ActaSeal are argued in public rather than asserted here. In the IETF SCITT working group:

  • Issue #461 — an evidence-binding trust-boundary finding on the architecture draft.
  • Pull request #463 — receipt profile requirements for verification results, with an implementation status section covering two independent implementations.
