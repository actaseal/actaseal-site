Shopify Payments is holding your payouts. Build the appeal.

A reserve, a payout hold or a disabled account, and the money you need for suppliers and staff is stuck. Type the facts from the email you got and from your own store. You get a ready appeal letter, a one-page record, the files to attach and the dates to follow up.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, and there is no signup. Leave a box empty if you do not know it; the letter leaves that line out.
