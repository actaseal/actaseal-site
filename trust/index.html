CUEC declaration

Complementary user-entity controls: what the operator of a deployment is responsible for.

ControlStatement
key_custodyActaSeal never holds a production signing key; the deployment operator is responsible for custody of whatever key is registered as active (KMS/HSM/offline).
access_review_cadenceActaSeal provides the mechanism to record an audit-trail review (who reviewed, what window); deciding who reviews and how often is the operator's own control.
retention_profileRetention duration is DATA the operator selects via a FrameworkProfile (or leaves undetermined); ActaSeal does not hardcode a retention period.
tsa_poolWhich RFC 3161 timestamp authorities are trusted is a caller-supplied pool; ActaSeal does not ship or hardcode a fixed TSA set the operator is bound to.
approver_role_assignmentWhich role is required to approve a given policy decision is operator-set policy data (ApprovalRule.required_role); ActaSeal enforces a mismatch (APPROVER_ROLE_MISMATCH) but does not choose or hardcode who holds that role.
network_component_selection_under_airgapWhether network-touching components (S3 Object Lock anchor, Rekor, RFC 3161 TSA pool, WorkOS/Logto SSO, SMTP/Resend email) are enabled is the operator's own ACTASEAL_AIRGAPPED choice; ActaSeal refuses each by name rather than silently degrading when air-gapped mode is set.
