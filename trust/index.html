CUEC declaration

Complementary user-entity controls: what the operator of a deployment is responsible for.

ControlStatement
key_custodyActaSeal never holds a production signing key; the deployment operator is responsible for custody of whatever key is registered as active (KMS/HSM/offline).
access_review_cadenceActaSeal provides the mechanism to record an audit-trail review (who reviewed, what window); deciding who reviews and how often is the operator's own control.
retention_profileHow long records are retained is the operator's choice, made by selecting a retention profile (or leaving it unset); ActaSeal does not impose a retention period of its own.
tsa_poolWhich RFC 3161 timestamp authorities are trusted is the operator's choice; ActaSeal does not bind the operator to a fixed set of timestamp authorities.
approver_role_assignmentWhich role is required to approve a given policy decision is set by the operator in the policy; ActaSeal refuses an approval from anyone who does not hold that role, but does not choose who holds it.
network_component_selection_under_airgapWhether network-touching components (S3 Object Lock anchor, Rekor, RFC 3161 TSA pool, WorkOS/Logto SSO, SMTP/Resend email) are enabled is the operator's choice; when the operator turns on air-gapped mode, ActaSeal refuses each by name rather than silently degrading.

Deletion and key compromise

Personal data in the sealed dispute-context section of a record can be erased by destroying the data subject's key; the ledger still verifies afterwards. Identifiers sent with an action, approver identity and free-text reasons cannot be erased. An active legal hold blocks erasure. See data retention and deletion for the full statement, and if a signing key is compromised for what happens to a key and the receipts it signed.
