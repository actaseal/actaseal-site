Price

Prices are in Indian rupees, per GSTIN, and exclude GST.

Refund audit

₹25,000 per GSTIN, per financial year.

  • You send your refund and credit note files. First findings within 48 hours.
  • You get a findings report, the rows behind it, draft credit notes, and a sealed record anyone can check.
  • Fee back: if the audit finds less than ₹25,000 at stake, you get the full fee back.

Monthly refund checks

₹4,999 per GSTIN a month, or ₹49,999 a year.

  • Your refunds re-checked every month, gaps flagged before the 30 November cut-off, drafts each month.
  • Month to month. No lock-in.

For CA firms

₹24,999 a year for up to 25 client GSTINs, ₹999 for each extra GSTIN.

  • Use it in your own engagements and bill your clients your own fee.

Try it first, on your own file

The refund check and the GST credit note check run in your browser on your own file. No signup, and the file does not leave your computer.

Who you pay

Invoices are issued by Xavier Goshi, Mumbai, India, who is accountable for ActaSeal. GST is added to the invoice once ActaSeal is GST-registered.

How to pay

  1. Write to sales@actaseal.com with your GSTIN, your company's billing name and which plan you want.
  2. You get an invoice by email within one working day. The payment details are on the invoice.
  3. Pay by UPI or bank transfer (NEFT, IMPS or RTGS) in Indian rupees.
  4. Send your files once the payment shows. For the refund audit, first findings come within 48 hours, and the fee back promise above still applies.

Only pay to the details on an invoice sent from an @actaseal.com address. If anyone sends you different details, write to security@actaseal.com before you pay.
