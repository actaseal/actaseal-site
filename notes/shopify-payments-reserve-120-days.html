Shopify Payments reserve for 120 days: what it means and when the money comes back

Short answer: a Shopify Payments reserve holds back a fixed sum, or a share of each sale, until the date in the reserve terms; 120 days is the example Shopify's own help page uses, not a fixed rule. Before that date Shopify reviews the account and may lift, keep, reduce or increase the reserve; if it is not extended, the held money is added to your upcoming payouts, which can take several business days.

What does a 120-day reserve on Shopify Payments mean?

Shopify holds a fixed sum, or a share of each sale, for the period in the reserve terms, to cover possible disputes and refunds. With a share-of-sales reserve, each sale is held for that period from when it was made, so money comes back sale by sale. Shopify's help page uses 120 days as its example; it does not state a standard length.

Can I still take orders while a reserve is on?

Usually yes. Shopify says a reserve generally does not stop you accepting payments through Shopify Payments unless you are told otherwise.

Where do I see how much is held and until when?

In the Shopify admin, go to Finance, then Payouts, then Reserve. The reserve message can show the reason, the date funds are held until and, for a share-of-sales reserve, the share held. You can export the reserve transactions as a CSV.

Can I appeal a Shopify Payments reserve?

To appeal, reply to the email with the reserve terms within the time it gives; some reserves cannot be appealed. If the email lists requirements for removal, the reserve is removed automatically once you meet them.

What happens when the 120 days end?

Shopify reviews the account before expiry and may lift, keep, reduce or increase the reserve. If no extension is needed, the reserved amount is added to upcoming payouts, and processing can take several business days.

Keep the reserve email, the CSV export and your dispute and refund numbers together: they are what an appeal reply rests on. You can build the appeal, the one-page record and the follow-up dates in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
