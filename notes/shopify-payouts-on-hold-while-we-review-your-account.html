"Your payouts from Shopify Payments are on hold while we review your account": what to do

Short answer: this is an account hold, not a reserve: Shopify stops your Shopify Payments payouts until the reason for the hold is resolved, and customers can usually still check out. The store owner acts on it, by replying to Shopify's email or answering the questions in the admin, as the notice asks; if nothing was asked for, contact Shopify Support.

Why are my Shopify Payments payouts on hold?

Shopify says holds are specific to each account. Possible causes include missing information, wrong bank account details and product eligibility, and also an unsupported business type, sanctions, a creditor's legal notice, and banking-partner review of high-value goods such as jewellery, watches or art.

Can my customers still check out during the hold?

Usually yes, but payouts for those orders are withheld until the hold is resolved. In some cases the store cannot accept new orders; the admin banner or the owner's email says which.

Who can resolve the hold?

The store owner: only the owner can see the account details and act on the hold. Staff may see a general notice or an email pointing them to the owner. A hold from a creditor's legal notice is the exception: only the payment processor can lift it.

I can't find Shopify's email. Where is it?

It goes to the store owner's email address, so check that inbox's spam folder. To see which address that is, go to Settings, then Users and permissions, and open the name under Store owner.

How long does a Shopify Payments account hold last?

Shopify's help page gives no time frame. The hold lasts until the requested information is provided and the review is finished; if the notice does not say what is needed, contact Shopify Support.

A hold caused by a creditor's legal notice is different: Shopify says only the payment processor can lift it, after a release from the creditor. For any other hold, write down the date it started, every ticket number and each document you sent; you can build the appeal, the one-page record and the follow-up dates in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Before clicking a link in a hold email or sharing bank details, check the email is really from Shopify: Shopify's own help page warns about phishing.
