GST credit notes: does every refund have one before 30 November?

Section 34(2) of the CGST Act says a credit note "shall" be declared in the return for the month it is issued, but not later than the thirtieth day of November following the end of the financial year in which the supply was made, or the date of the annual return if that is earlier. The November date replaced September from 1 October 2022.

For a brand that refunds orders, the GST on a refunded sale comes back only through that credit note. For last year's sales, the date is 30 November 2026. After it, the GST on a refund with no credit note stays paid. A proviso also denies the reduction where the tax was passed on to someone else.

Small intra-state sales to consumers are reported in GSTR-1 as one B2CS total, so their credit notes do not show there one by one. The credit note register your books keep is where a missing one shows.

The question for finance is plain: for every order you refunded last year, is there a credit note, for the full amount, declared in time?

This is a reading of the law, not tax advice.
