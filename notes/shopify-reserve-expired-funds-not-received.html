Shopify reserve expired but funds not received: what to check

Short answer: when a reserve ends without being extended, Shopify adds the held money to your upcoming payouts and processing can take several business days, then your bank can take more. If no Reserved Funds release shows in your payout transactions after the expiry date, the reserve may have been kept or extended at the review Shopify does before expiry, or payouts may be paused by an account hold or a recent payout-account change: reply to the reserve email and ask for the new date and the reason.

My Shopify reserve expired. Why haven't I received the money?

Shopify reviews the account before a reserve expires and may lift, keep, reduce or increase it. If it is lifted, the amount is added to upcoming payouts, and processing can take several business days before it is sent to your bank.

How long after the reserve ends should the money reach my bank?

Shopify's Shopify Balance help page says a transfer from Shopify Payments to an external account can take up to 5 business days, not counting weekends and US holidays, and banks can need up to 3 more days. Timing varies by region and bank.

Where do I check whether the reserve was released?

In the Shopify admin, go to Finance, then Payouts, click View transactions and filter by the Reserved Funds transaction type. A released reserve shows there as reserve transactions paid back into payouts.

Why do I see a negative reserve transaction after an early release?

If a reserve is released early, you may get one bulk positive reserve transaction. On the original expiry date the original amounts are paid out with matching negative reserve transactions, so the early release is not counted twice.

What if nothing has arrived and support doesn't answer?

Reply to the reserve terms email, quote its expiry date and amount, and ask for the release date or the reason it was kept. Keep each reply dated, so every follow-up can point to how long you have waited.

Shopify gives no exact count for the release; start counting from the expiry date in the reserve terms, not from the day you noticed. You can build the appeal, the one-page record and the follow-up dates in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
