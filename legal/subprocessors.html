Changes

We will update this page before adding a sub-processor, and will tell existing customers by email. If you object to an addition, you may terminate for that reason and we will refund the unused portion of your term.

What is deliberately not on this list

There is no cloud provider holding your data, because we do not hold your data. There is no analytics provider, no customer data platform, no AI vendor processing your support material, and no CDN serving this site's assets. If any of those change, this page changes first.
